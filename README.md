# sales-analytics-ai-lab

Practical AI engineering frameworks for sales analytics, account growth, and market intelligence. Turn trusted data and verified signals into useful decisions, relevant conversations, and measurable output.

## Status

Initial project blueprint. The examples below are planned; this repository does not yet include runnable implementations or measured productivity results. Gridbrief is an existing external reference site, not source code bundled with this repository.

## Featured reference: Gridbrief through OpenAI Sites

[Gridbrief: Enterprise Capital Intelligence](https://gridbrief-ai-infrastructure.talldata.chatgpt.site/) demonstrates a public intelligence surface for tracking major investments and their local implications. The lab will document the repeatable workflow behind using a site like this to serve and connect with a target audience.

The proposed workflow is: collect a signal, verify its source and date, match it to an audience need, publish a useful insight through OpenAI Sites, prepare a relevant conversation starter, and measure the response. Publishing and outreach require separate approval. A public announcement is evidence of a market development, not proof of buying intent.

## Shared engineering frameworks

- Trusted metrics: calculate numbers in SQL or code against documented definitions.
- Context assembly: supply relevant records, account history, dates, and sources.
- Evidence to action: distinguish facts, hypotheses, and proposed actions.
- Human review: route uncertain matches and consequential actions for review.
- Evaluation: compare accuracy, usefulness, human effort, coverage, and cost with a manual baseline.

## Measuring output

Treat 100x output as a testable ambition, not a performance claim. Measure coverage separately from accepted outputs and business outcomes. Include review and correction time in human effort. Synthetic tests demonstrate behavior; they do not establish real-world revenue lift.
